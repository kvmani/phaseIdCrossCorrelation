# Scientific Citation Policy

Scientific claims, convention choices, and mathematically important formulations should be cited where doing so materially helps interpretation or prevents ambiguity.

## Cite when

- a convention is nontrivial or easy to misuse
- a workflow rests on established literature rather than a purely local implementation detail
- a visualization or orientation-coloring rule has a known scientific reference
- algorithm interpretation depends on prior published context

## Do not over-cite

Not every sentence needs a citation. The goal is scientific clarity, not decorative bibliography density.
